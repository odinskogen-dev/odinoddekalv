About

AMM is the artistic practice of Amalie Myrtvedt.

Her drawings bring together handmade line, colour and figurative worlds populated by animals and people.

This is a selection from a growing body of work. Original artworks, editions, artistic projects and collaborations can be discussed directly with the studio. Titles, material details and availability are confirmed individually.
